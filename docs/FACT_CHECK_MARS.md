## Wording choices made to avoid overclaiming
- Viking 1: "NASA's first spacecraft to land safely on Mars" (not "first on Mars": a Soviet lander touched down in 1971 but failed within seconds).
- Sojourner: "NASA's first rover on Mars."
- Spirit: "kept going for six years" (it drove for about 5, then sat stuck and still talking until 2010).
- Opportunity: "when I stopped, it was farther than any rover had ever driven on another world" (true in 2018; not claimed as a record today).
- InSight laser mirror: stated only that it carries one (NSSDCA). We do not claim anyone has pinged it.
- Viking life experiments: deliberately left out. Results are still debated, and a kid-sized answer would overclaim either way.
